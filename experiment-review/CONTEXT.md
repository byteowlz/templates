# Review-gallery vocabulary

- **Exhibit:** one curated experiment and its conclusion; not a filesystem
  directory, training fold, or archive.
- **Comparison:** matched media with the same timing, canvas and known baseline.
- **Evidence:** a source still/native view/sheet that tests the conclusion.
- **Hold:** a rights or training restriction that must remain visible wherever
  an exhibit appears.
- **Demo media:** synthetic, locally authored illustrations only. Replace with
  separately reviewed assets when adapting this template.
